*The great city, which should be reined thousands of years ago, but forgiven.*
The place where all stories happen.
**Location:** We don't know where it is. 

We don't know if Nineveh is located in our continent, our planet or our universe.
We do't even know if Nineveh has its "outside".

The city looks like an enlarged downtown of Manhattan. Both modern buildings and ancient religious buildings co-exist in the same place densely.

Churches, research institutes, colleges, hospitals and commercial areas were mixed up in the city, stringed by roads and urban railways.

The city was constructed to solve [[003-002 The Five Unrevealed Mysteries]].
Science and theology never become two. 

Studies on **[[003-002 The Five Unrevealed Mysteries]]** brought new technologies and modern lifestyles to the citizens, most of whom do not care about the problems in their daily life.
Pop cultures are available in Nineveh, and it is quite popular.

Heresy is wrong, but not a crime in Nineveh.
Punishment of heresy includes orthodox education, detention and voluntary labor.
Severe crime refers to substantial damage to conditions for the Teleological Argument, destroying irreplaceable evidences or obstructions to key studies.
