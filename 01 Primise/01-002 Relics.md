**Relics** are items, structures or phenomenons which can be observed or used reproducibly, not cannot be explained by modern science in [[01-001 Nineveh City]].

**Relics** may not be holy or dangerous.

**Relics** can be a *World-class OOPArt* such as an "Anti-matter shotgun" or a "Perfect Toaster" which can make perfect toast without any electrical power.

The research and religious institutions are in charge of registration, investigation, preservation and controlling of the Relics.

There are unregistered Relics.
