**Core Theme:** Childlike Spirit  
**Identity:** [[01-003 Almah]]; licensed Hierarch and Mystery Keeper of the Church of the Sevenfold Holy Way, holding the rare title of **Grand Mystery Keeper**.

### **Appearance**

Inspired primarily by _Through the Looking-Glass_. Blue, white, and gold palette; mirrors, symmetry, chess motifs, and a pet cat named **Cherub**. Exact age and outfit remain undefined.

### **Personality**

Mysterious, humble, curious, sincere, playful, and exceptionally knowledgeable. Her childlike nature reflects genuine intellectual openness, not immaturity or ignorance.

She explains complex theories through fairy tales, numerology, and deliberately imperfect analogies, often dismissing her own explanations as **“That’s all nonsense.”** She respects criticism, readily admits mistakes, gets genuinely upset when defeated, but forgives quickly.

### **Background & Role**

A legitimate, highly accomplished scholar specializing in **Angelic Order**. Her accessible explanations charm ordinary people but frustrate academic peers for their lack of rigor.

Her research helped launch the field of artificially reproducing the **Almah phenomenon**, contributing to the rise of **Project SAPERE** and earning her Grand Mystery Keeper title. However, SAPERE researchers seemingly overlooked a crucial statement in her work:

**“Even an Almah within the finite cannot reach the Greatest Kingdom.”**

Its precise meaning and implications remain unresolved.

### **Companion**

**Cherub:** A cat Alice treats as an intelligent conversational partner. Whether the cat actually communicates or possesses supernatural qualities is unknown.

### **Signature Lines**

- **Introduction:** “Alice Chesser, Grand Mystery Keeper of the Sevenfold Holy Way, at your service.”
- **Before Battle:** “Then, pardon me.”
- **Victory:** “Checkmate~”
- **Defeat:** “I’ll turn you into a kitten!”
- **Catchphrase:** “We are all mad here.”
- **On Analogies:** “It’s all nonsense.”

**Essence:** A brilliant, genuinely qualified scholar who explains cosmic mysteries through childish metaphors, listens seriously to her cat, and preserves her innocent curiosity despite profound knowledge.
