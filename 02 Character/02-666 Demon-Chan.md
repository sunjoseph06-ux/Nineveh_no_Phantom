
# **Real Name: Planostriathea Malefica Vesperis-Vileth**

**Title:** Empress of the Lost Horizon  
**Police Codename:** Abyss  
**Core Theme:** A cute demon
**Identity:** Unknown. Possibly a heretical researcher, subculture fanatic,[[01-003 Almah]], genuine demon, or the same entity recorded thousands of years ago.

### **Appearance**

A corrupted icon assembled from theology, pop culture, urban occultism, and personal taste.

Black, wine-red, purple-black, dark gray, and tarnished metal, accented by aggressively bright neon colors. Her clothing mixes religious fragments, jirai/street fashion, idol or live-house aesthetics, and practical academic/technical wear.

She has horns, but whether they are biological, artificial, ornamental, or something stranger remains unclear. Her appearance should feel **so fake that it loops back into seeming real**.

### **Personality**

Fast-talking, theatrical, provocative, intensely curious, and extremely human. She genuinely loves memes, idols, horror films, urban legends, cheap religious souvenirs, limited-edition merchandise, tacky accessories, caffeine, and whatever trend appeared last week.

Her interests constantly change, but the underlying Almah-like self remains stable: **she is eternally obsessed with whatever feels new, shiny, fashionable, or exciting right now.**

She enjoys monopolizing the stage, offending theologians, and turning conversations into philosophical traps, but her persona is not empty performance.

### **Danger**

Not evil, but genuinely dangerous.

She casually appropriates forbidden theological language, most notoriously declaring:

**“I AM THAT I AM.”**

Behind the theatrics lies real knowledge. She may have stolen classified material, infiltrated laboratories, studied forbidden texts, once belonged to an orthodox institution, or personally encountered something beyond accepted doctrine.

Her specialty is **self-reference, self-naming, identity, and the forbidden seizure of the first person:** Who may say “I am”? Can a finite being legitimately claim absoluteness?

The disturbing part is not that she talks nonsense. It is that some of the nonsense appears to be true.

### **Mirror to the Ultimate AI SAPERE** 

SPERE is an **answer deliberately produced by institutions**.  
Demon-Chan is a **question that grew in the cracks between them**.

Where the AI is pure, restrained, precise, and icon-like, she is excessive, contaminated, provocative, and unmistakably human. She is not its opposite, but its **dirty mirror**.

### **The Abyss**

[[003-911 NCPD]] files identify her as **Abyss**, inspiring the running joke:

**“Gaze long enough into the Abyss, and the Abyss gazes back.”**

Investigators assigned to her case eventually either lose patience or start helping refine her theories.

Her absurdly long name is already avoided in ordinary speech. Ancient records contain an even longer suspected ritual title:

**“Her Abyssal Majesty, Planostriathea Malefica Vesperis-Vileth, The Sovereign of Lost Paths, Weaver of the Great Delusion, and Architect of the False Dawn.”**

Nobody has been eager to test what happens if it is recited in full.

### **Essence**

A girl who seems simultaneously like a jirai girl, occult influencer, heretical scholar, subculture addict, urban legend, fake demon, and real demon.

**At first, she looks ridiculous. Then you suspect she knows something. Eventually, you realize it may be safer not to let her finish speaking.**
