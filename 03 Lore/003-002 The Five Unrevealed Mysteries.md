[[003-001 The Church]] openly acknowledges that its theology remains incomplete.

**A doctrine that can be proven glorifies God more greatly than one that merely demands belief.**

Its most sacred field of theological investigation is therefore called:
**Unrevealed Theology**  
_Theologia Non-Revelata_

This discipline is organized around five fundamental problems.

## **First Mystery: The Problem of Divine Generation**
Why must the infinite whole possess nontrivial reflexive structure?
If so, it would constitute a mathematical-theological proof of the eternal generation of the Son.

## **Second Mystery: The Problem of Law**
How can a genuine partial order be derived solely from a primitive binary relation without secretly presupposing difference, inclusion, magnitude, or precedence?
The Church calls this:
**The Question of Moses**

Because it concerns how Law can arise from God.

## **Third Mystery: The Problem of Creation**
What forms of relational closure genuinely constitute a finite local structure?
This is known as:
**The Formal Conditions of Genesis.**

## **Fourth Mystery: The Problem of the Kingdom**
If relational closure can be expressed as a fixed point:
F(X)=X
Then what are the respective meanings of \mu F and \nu F?

One theological school argues:
- The least fixed point corresponds to the minimal self-consistent created structure.
- The greatest fixed point corresponds to the divine whole.
Another school argues that this interpretation still illegitimately projects finite mathematical tools onto the infinite.

The controversy is called:
**The Dispute Between the Least Kingdom and the Greatest Kingdom.**

## **Fifth Mystery: The Problem of the Soul**
How can purely relational structures rigorously give rise to subjective experience, self-representation, memory, temporal awareness and first-person subjectivity?
This is the only question in the entire religion capable of making theologians, mathematicians, neuroscientists, and monks equally irritated with one another.
It is therefore known as:
**The Final Veil.**

## **A short version:**
1. Why nontrivial reflexive structures must exist?
2. How partial orders can be derived from primitive binary relations?
3. How finite local structures can be rigorously defined?
4. How least and greatest fixed points should be interpreted?
5. How consciousness can emerge from relational structure?
