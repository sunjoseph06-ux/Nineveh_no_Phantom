The religion’s clerical hierarchy can deliberately combine ancient traditions with academic institutions.
Ordinary local clergy retain familiar titles such as Deacon, Priest and Bishop.
But advanced theological institutions recognize three additional offices.

## **Exegetes**
Scholars who investigate how traditional scriptures correspond to the Seven Sacred Orders.

## **Hierologists**
Specialists in mathematics, logic, physics, and relational ontology.
They believe that proving a new theorem can itself constitute a form of prayer.

## **Mystery Keeper**
Scholars dedicated specifically to the Five Unrevealed Mysteries.
